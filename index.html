Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Vehicle Fitness Certificates

What is a Vehicle Fitness Certificate?

A mandatory document certifying your vehicle is roadworthy and meets safety standards as per the Motor Vehicle Act.

Who needs to apply?

All commercial vehical including goods carriage, passenger vehicles, and taxis that are over 2 years old.

What is the validity period?

New vehicle: 2 years. Vehicle older than 2 years: 1 year, must be renewed anually.

How long does inspection take?

Physical inspection typically takes 30-60 minutes. Certificate issued within 24 hours of successful inspection.

Can I reschedule my slot?

Yes, you can reschedule up to 24 hours before the schedule time via the Track Application section.

What if my vehicle fails inspection?

You receive a detailed deficiency report. Re-apply afetr fixing issues within 30 days at a reduced fee.
